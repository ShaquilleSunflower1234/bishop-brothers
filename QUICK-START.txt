BISHOP BROTHERS 1.0.0 — FREE WINDOWS APP

1. Extract the ZIP and open BishopBrothers.exe.
2. Plug in your Xbox 360 thumb drive. The app finds your boxer save.
   Or drop your copied Content folder into the app.
3. Select your profile/save if more than one appears.
4. Click Add Bishops and wait for Done.

The app backs up, merges, signs, verifies, and puts the save back automatically.
No Horizon, signing setup, or payment is needed.

Afterward: safely eject USB. On Xbox 360, sign into the same account and open
Settings > System > Storage > USB > Games and Apps > Fight Night Champion.
Copy Fight Night Champion Boxers to Cloud Saved Games. Wait for upload.
On Xbox One, launch the game using the same account and let it sync.

You need an EXISTING Fight Night Champion Boxers save. If missing, edit an
available boxer in the game, save, then copy that boxer save to USB on Xbox 360.
Champion Mode completion is not required just to create a boxer-edit save.

Backups: click Open backups in the app.
Your original save is kept in each export's Original backup folder.
The app works locally and does not upload saves or collect payments.

Supported: the observed native Windows Content-folder/save layout described
in README.md. Older FATX image USB formats and unknown save schemas are not
supported. The owner reported success in an Xbox test on October 14, 2026;
other setups may differ. Keep your backup until gameplay checks pass.
